# Privacy and review moderation notes

This website includes contact/booking links and a review form powered by Supabase. The review form may collect a visitor's **name**, **selected service**, **rating**, and **review text**.

- Do not request or disclose medical histories, contact details, or patient-identifying information in public reviews.
- Submitted reviews are intended to remain pending until approved by the site administrator.
- Visitors should see only approved reviews; verify this against live Supabase RLS policies.
- The owner is responsible for giving users appropriate collection notices, handling deletion requests and defining a retention policy.
- External third parties such as WhatsApp, Netlify, Supabase, fonts and CDN providers apply their own data processing practices.

This file is an engineering checklist, **not** a formal legal privacy policy. Publish an appropriate site privacy notice before gathering personal information in production.
